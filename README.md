# PaceCraft — Marathon Pace & Split Calculator 🏃💨

A modern marathon and endurance race pacing application built with pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Features
- **Goal Finish Time Controls**: Interactive digital race clock and fast presets (Sub 3:00 to 5:00).
- **Multiple Distances**: Full Marathon (42.195 km / 26.22 mi), Half Marathon (21.1 km), 10K, and 5K.
- **Pacing Strategies**: Even Pace, Negative Split (faster 2nd half), and Positive Split with adjustable variance.
- **Detailed Splits**: Complete split breakdown by kilometer and mile, with major gates and "The Wall" (30K) checkpoints.
- **⚡ Energy & Fuel Reminders**: Energy gel and hydration reminders mapped along the course.
- **Interactive Pace Curve**: SVG visualization of pacing progression.
- **Printable Race Wristband**: Race-day wristband generator ready to print or copy.
- **Export to CSV**: Download splits for training analysis and watch pacing bands.

## Tech Stack
- Vanilla HTML5
- Modern Vanilla CSS (Dark runner aesthetic, neon volt & cyan accents, glassmorphism)
- Pure Vanilla JavaScript (No dependencies, zero build steps)
